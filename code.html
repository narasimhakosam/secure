Common Scam Red Flags at a Glance

Legitimate employers and university programs follow transparent hiring frameworks. Watch for these signals:

payments

Upfront Payment Demands

Legitimate companies never ask candidates to pay for training kits, laptop security deposits, or file processing.

flag High Risk Signal (+35)

Receiving an instant appointment letter or 100% stipend guarantee without any technical test or telephonic screen.
